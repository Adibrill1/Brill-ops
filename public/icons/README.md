# App icons — placeholders

`icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` and `icon-maskable-512.png` are **placeholders**: a plain
white "BO" monogram on the site's ink colour (`#0f172a`), generated by
`scripts/generate-placeholder-icons.py`.

They exist so the PWA manifest resolves and "Add to Home Screen" produces a real icon
rather than a broken one. They are not branding — no Brill Ops brand assets were supplied
with the handoff.

**To replace:** export the real logo at 192×192 and 512×512, plus a 512×512 maskable
version with the artwork inside the central ~80% safe zone (Android crops icons to a
circle). Drop them here with the same filenames and delete the generator script. Nothing
else needs changing — `manifest.webmanifest` already points at these names.

`src/app/icon.png` is the favicon and is a copy of the 192px file; update it too.
